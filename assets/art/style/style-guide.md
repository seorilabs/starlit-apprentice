# 별빛 견습생 스타일 가이드

Flat vector illustration in a storybook picture-book style, drawn with a very dark near-black outline (#0E1120) of even, heavy weight on every edge including interior details.

Shading is strictly flat: each surface uses exactly two tones — one base and one shadow — with a hard edge between them. Absolutely no gradients, no glossy sheen, no specular highlights, no airbrush, no texture, no grain, no noise.

Use only this palette: #161B2D night navy, #1E2540 deep panel blue, #F4F1E8 warm parchment, #F2C75C starlight gold, #3E8E8A muted teal, #D97A7A dusty rose. Skin and hair may use at most two additional warm supporting tones. Do not introduce any other hue — no bright cyan, no pastel pink, no saturated orange.

A single warm light source sits at the upper left, as if from a lantern or starlight, so shadow tones fall to the lower right.

Proportions are rounded, friendly and slightly stylized. Mood is a quiet nighttime academy — calm, warm, ink and parchment, unhurried.

Centered single subject, isolated on a flat pure white background. No ground shadow, no cast shadow, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no watermark, no border, no frame.
